## 3. Audio & Voiceover Engineering Specifications

1. **Master Voice Profile:**
   - **Voice Name/Type:** "Aditi" (Warm, articulate, bilingual Indian-English accent, natural breath control, enthusiastic but composed).
   - **Pitch & Speed:** Speed `1.02x` (natural conversational cadence), Pitch `0.00` (neutral, warm resonant midrange).
2. **Audio Post-Processing Chain:**
   - **High-Pass Filter:** 80Hz (18dB/octave slope) to remove rumble.
   - **De-Esser:** -3dB cut at 6.2kHz to suppress harsh sibilance.
   - **Multiband Compressor:** 3:1 ratio on mids, soft knee for broadcast presence.
   - **Integrated Loudness:** Target `-14.0 LUFS` (+/- 0.5 LUFS) with true peak ceiling at `-1.0 dBFS`.
3. **Music & Ducking Automation:**
   - Music bed at `-12 dBFS` during intro (00:00-00:02).
   - Dynamic sidechain ducking drops music to `-22 dBFS` whenever narration is active (attack: `15ms`, release: `250ms`).
   - Music swells to `-10 dBFS` during the climax (00:58-01:08) and resolves at 01:15.

## 4. Stitching & Rendering Assembly Strategy

### Option A: Local FFmpeg Stitching Script (Deterministic, Zero-Cloud)
Use the local FFmpeg toolchain to assemble the rendered 7 clips with cross-fades and burned subtitles:

```bash
# Example FFmpeg xfade transition filter between consecutive clips:
ffmpeg -i clip1.mp4 -i clip2.mp4 -i clip3.mp4 -i clip4.mp4 -i clip5.mp4 -i clip6.mp4 -i clip7.mp4 \
  -i master_voiceover_bgm.wav \
  -filter_complex \
  "[0:v][1:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=9.5[v01]; \
   [v01][2:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=21.0[v12]; \
   [v12][3:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=32.5[v23]; \
   [v23][4:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=44.0[v34]; \
   [v34][5:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=55.5[v45]; \
   [v45][6:v]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.5:offset=65.0[vfinal]; \
   [vfinal]subtitles=appu_promo.ass[vout]" \
  -map "[vout]" -map 7:a -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 18 -c:a aac -b:a 320k \
  appu_promo_75s_master.mp4
```
