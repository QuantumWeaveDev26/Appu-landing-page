Frequently Asked Questions

How do subscription renewals work?

All paid plans renew automatically at the end of each billing month or year via Razorpay. You can cancel renewals at any time from your Parent Zone dashboard with zero penalties.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing intervals anytime from the Parent Zone. Changes will take effect in your next billing cycle.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept all major payment methods across India via Razorpay, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Netbanking across all major Indian banks, and Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay).
