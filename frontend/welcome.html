Why is the sky blue?

A

Great question! Sunlight looks white, but it is really a mix of every colour.

  1. When sunlight enters the air, it bumps into tiny gas molecules.
  2. Blue light has a short wavelength, so it scatters the most.
  3. That scattered blue light reaches your eyes from every direction.
