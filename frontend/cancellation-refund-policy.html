Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms and procedures for managing and cancelling subscriptions on the APPU AI Learning Companion platform (appuai.online), operated by IGR Academy.

Effective Date: August 25, 2026
Last Updated: August 25, 2026
Service Model: Digital Cloud Subscription

Digital Subscription Summary

APPU is a real-time digital AI educational service. Paid subscription entitlements (such as AI learning sessions, voice practice minutes, and advanced personalization) are provisioned digitally and immediately upon successful payment verification via Razorpay.

1. Subscription Cancellation

Parents and account holders may cancel their recurring subscription at any time directly through the platform:

  • How to Cancel: Log in to your parent account, navigate to the Parent Zone • Setup • Subscription tab, and select the cancellation option.
  • Effect of Cancellation: When you cancel a recurring plan, automatic renewal for subsequent billing cycles is immediately stopped. You will not be charged for subsequent billing periods.
  • Access Until Period End: Your learner will continue to have full, uninterrupted access to their paid subscription entitlements until the end of the current pre-paid billing cycle (monthly or annual).
  • No Cancellation Fees: We do not levy any penalty or administrative charge for cancelling your subscription.

2. Refund Policy for Consumed Services

Because APPU provides immediate digital access and incurs compute costs for real-time AI and voice model generation:

  • Payments for completed, pre-paid monthly or annual subscription periods that have already commenced are generally non-refundable once active digital access has been provisioned and utilized.
  • We encourage parents to experience the complimentary discovery access and the APPU Free plan before upgrading to a paid subscription tier.

3. Technical Billing Errors & Duplicate Charges

We are committed to fair and transparent billing. Refunds will be promptly investigated and processed in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate Transactions: If your bank account or card was debited more than once for the same subscription cycle due to a network glitch or gateway timeout.
  • Payment Deducted but Access Not Activated: If a payment succeeded on Razorpay but our system encountered an unrecoverable technical error and failed to activate your subscription plan within 24 hours.
  • Unauthorized Charges: Proven fraudulent or unauthorized transactions reported with appropriate verification.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

For approved technical refunds:

  • Refunds are initiated via the original payment method through the Razorpay payment gateway.
  • Standard banking turnaround times in India typically range from 5 to 7 business days for the credit to reflect in your original bank account, UPI linked account, or card statement.

5. How to Submit a Refund Request

To request a review or resolution for a billing discrepancy, please submit a request to our support desk with the following details:

  • Registered Parent Email Address
  • Razorpay Payment ID (e.g. pay_...) or Transaction Reference Number
  • Date and Amount of Transaction (in INR)
  • Brief description of the billing discrepancy
